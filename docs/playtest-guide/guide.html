Lionheart Fixt · 0.1.4-rc1

The Horde Playtest Route

Everything 0.1.0 through 0.1.4 changed, ordered by where you walk rather than by feature. Eleven stops, two characters, and the Crossroads twice — because the contract needs a standing you cannot have on the first pass.

Start a new game. Both times.

Two independent blockers, both confirmed the hard way:

  • New map entities never appear on a save that already entered that level. The entity list is captured the first time a level is visited and restored from that snapshot forever. This hides the Goblin Girl, the Goblin Guards, Hub'blub's second store, the Patrol Leader's conversation, the goblins' new cursor and the Khan's Champion greeting.
  • New dialogue nodes do not retrofit onto a conversation a character has already had.

Edits to existing dialogue text are picked up on revisit, which is what makes this trap dangerous — it builds false confidence that revisiting works.

Save at each threshold once and reuse those saves: outside Hrubjub's wall, the Crossroads, the goblin camp gate, inside Goblin Warrens, the Lake.

Two constraints worth planning around. Rakeb needs Tribal 80 and Grumdjum needs Thought 80 — two different schools on one character. If A can only afford one, take either and record which you skipped.

And Esteban is spell-immune. A caster cannot damage him at any level.

How standing works now

Goblin rank accumulates. Every service to the Horde grants the next rank you do not have, so any three of them in any order reach rank 3 and none is ever wasted. There is no required first step and no required order.

any service
rank 0 → Goblin Chum  ·  rank 1 → Goblin Blooded  ·  rank 2 → Goblin Champion  ·  rank 3 → nothing
the five
Hrubjub's spy quest · the vodyanoi for Rakeb · the River Dryad for Grumdjum · the woodcutter's eyes · the Everlasting to the Khan
rank 1
opens the camp gate, Hub'blub's chum prices, the daughter's guard, the Patrol Leader's recognition, and every villager greeting
rank 2
the Esteban contract, and Rakeb speaking to you as clan
rank 3
the Khan's Champion greeting, Rakeb's misgivings, and villagers kneeling
meeting the Khan
recorded separately — it changes what the Patrol Leader says at rank 1

This is worth deliberately testing out of order, because it was broken until late: rank used to be granted by Hrubjub alone, so a player who never met him finished at rank 0 no matter how much they did for the Horde.

What 0.1.4 fixed, and what it did not

read this first

0.1.4 exists entirely because of the last playtest. Eight defects, and the ones that mattered were invisible to every static check — the files were byte-correct and the logic was wrong. Every one of its fixes has now been seen working, including the faction ranks, which were wrong for longer than anything else in the project.

  • A Take three goblin services and watch your standing.
    It must reach rank 3. Every tier used to grant +1 and each replaced the last, so standing never passed 1 and the Crossroads contract refused players who had earned it. Confirmed in play. If the contract ever refuses you at Goblin Blooded again, this has regressed. F1
  • A Compare a rank's bonuses against the one below.
    Each tier is now the running total of everything beneath it, so promotion never takes something away. Becoming Champion used to cost you Blooded's disease resistance and drop your Barter. F2
  • A Rakeb, at three different points in his errands.
    Take the eyes job and return without them → “You return, but we do not see the eyes.” Kill the devil fish and return before taking a second job → “I have a job for you.” Finish everything → “We hope the items have served you well.” All three were written and unreachable; he gave one generic line for every state. The “we do not see the eyes” rung is confirmed in play; the other two are not. Needs a character who has never entered the Warrens. F3
  • Both Ask the goblin girl for a pie twice, and Rakeb for the devil fish job twice.
    Neither should be given twice. Both were farmable. F4
  • Both Kill Guard Esteban, then return to the patrol leader.
    He pays, the quest completes, and Esteban's own quests fail. Confirmed working. It took three attempts: a corpse still exists, so the check had to ask whether he was alive. F5
  • Both Read the rank titles you have been given.
    Each describes your standing, not a deed. Goblin Blooded used to claim you butchered a woodcutter for his eyes even if you earned it by killing the river dryad. F6

The patrol that no longer charges

Crossroads · first pass

The order matters. Esteban's own goblin quest turns the patrol hostile — that's the choice working, not a bug — so parley first. You will not be offered the contract yet; that needs rank 2, and you come back at stop 10.

  • Both Ask Esteban about the local dangers. Do not accept his goblin quest. Walk toward the goblins.
    They do not attack. In vanilla this was an ambush. X1
  • Both Mouse over any goblin in the patrol.
    A speech cursor, not a sword. Clicking gets a line of banter. X1d
  • Both Talk to the Goblin Patrol Leader.
    A conversation opens. In vanilla, and in the first 0.1.1 build, the only interaction was an attack. X1b
  • B Talk with no Horde rank.
    The purge line, a Speech route, a fight and a walk-away. No recognition. X3
  • A Talk at rank 1, before meeting the Khan.
    He recognises you but offers no contract — “A chum who has carried one message down one road.” He sends you to the Khan. X4
  • Both Esteban: ask about monsters, then say goodbye.
    Reaches “Be safe traveler.” instead of dead-ending. The one repaired link you can observe. F4
  • B Now accept Esteban's goblin quest, look at the patrol, then attack.
    They turn hostile — vanilla's goblin confrontation relay, and the choice working. Then Patrol Leader, Scout and corner goblins must all turn. Any that stands inert means goblins attack failed to re-arm it. X1c, X2

The warrens

Wilderness · Goblin Warrens

Entering this map crashed the game until very late in the build — a node reference that differed from the shipped one by a single trailing space. If you get a “Fatal Not Found Error” on entry, that regressed.

  • Both Find the Goblin Girl near the Khan's court, and the two guards on the southern approach.
    Both ship finished with zero map references — they have never been in the game before. R1, R7
  • Both Meet her for the very first time with the River Dryad already dead — and again on the other character with the woodcutter dead.
    “You're cute for a... whatever it is you are.” Not the snails line, and not the woodsman line. Reported from play: she used to greet a total stranger as an old friend, because the greeting keyed on world state and never asked whether she had met you. T9–T10
  • Both Now talk to her again, and again after the woodcutter dies.
    Return line, then the snails if the dryad is dead, then 200 Returning after killing the woodsman. State greetings apply only once she knows you. R2–R3, R11, T11
  • Both Give her the liver, take the pie. With PE 7+ or IN 7+, smell it first.
    Named Liver Pie, correct icon, vanilla description. The check adds knowledge, not an item — you still get the pie. P1–P5
  • B Slot the pie on the hotkey belt and eat it at full health.
    Poison ticks for about a minute. In vanilla the item could not be slotted at all. P6–P7
  • B Talk to a villager at rank 0.
    The vanilla threat — “You look like a walking meal to me” — and only the Speech way out. T1
  • A Talk to a villager with any rank, then again at rank 3.
    At any rank the spear comes down: “your name has been in three mouths this week.” At rank 3 the goblin kneels: “Forgive it, Champion.” T2–T3
  • A Rakeb. Ask how he tells the future, then answer in his own vocabulary.
    Tribal 80 → 35 fellow practitioner. On B, only “I don't speak Goblin”. C6–C7
  • A Rakeb at rank 2, then at rank 3.
    At 2: “Clan. Yes. The bones have been saying so for a while.” At 3 he is not sure the clan should be glad — “a door left open, and doors are how weather gets in.” T4–T5
  • Both Clear the vodyanoi for Rakeb, and bring him the woodcutter's eyes.
    Each advances you one rank. Do the eyes as your first ever service and you become Goblin Chum, not Blooded — the cascade grants the next rank you lack. G6, G7
  • A The Khan. Reach rank 3 without ever handing him the Everlasting — spy, dryad and eyes alone get you there — then enter his cave.
    The vanilla greeting. He must not say the Everlasting hangs on his wall because you put it there. That line was gated on rank 3 alone, which was true only while rank 3 could only come from the Everlasting; accumulation made it reachable without him. Same bug as the Goblin Girl's, found by looking for its twin. T12
  • A Now deliver the Everlasting and speak to him again.
    The Champion greeting: “Not 'morsel'. Not today.” Still the morsel line means the map-side gate failed. T6, T13
  • A When he demands entertainment, at rank 3 and below.
    At rank 3 you can refuse: “Ask the room whether the Khan's champion dances.” Below it, the vanilla Speech 25 and the Schmooze route both still work. T7–T8, C8–C9
  • A Tell him about Grumdjum's poem.
    Available because the Schmooze route at the Lake set the same flag the vanilla routes set. C17

The Everlasting

Bounty Hunter Camp → Goblin Warrens

Haggle before you hand it over. Talk the weapon up first and he raises the payment; hand it over meekly and he lowballs you, then tells you why: “that was before you lost your bargaining… edge.” Push for more after he has already improved the offer and he eats you. None of that affects rank.

  • A Deal with the bounty hunter, take the Everlasting to the Khan.
    Advances one rank — Goblin Champion if you were at rank 2. G8
  • A Try the other two haggle prices afterwards.
    Rank does not increment again. The “I'm glad we see eye-to-eye” reply briefly advanced twice. G9, G7d
  • A Do a fourth and fifth service after reaching rank 3.
    Nothing happens. No rank, no duplicate title perk. G7c
  • A Check the sheet.
    Tiers stack: Sneak +30, Barter +14, Poison +35, Disease +10, Agility +1, carry weight +30. Three title perks. G10–G11
  • B Hand over the Everlasting with no Horde rank.
    You become Goblin Chum, and the shipped Goblin Champion perk is still granted as vanilla's reward for the deed. N6

Back for the contract

Crossroads · second pass

Esteban is 200 HP, 200 AC, melee 90, and spell-immune. You want One-Handed Melee around 90 and the attack-speed slider at its slowest — the formula divides your skill by 0.45 there against 0.8 at the fastest, worth more than several levels. Roughly level 9–12. If you can beat the Goblin Khan, you can beat him.

  • A Optional: talk to the Patrol Leader at rank 1 after meeting the Khan.
    “You have stood in front of the Khan and you are still only a chum.” He points you at the shaman's work. Still no contract. X4b
  • A Return at rank 2.
    The contract at last. With IN 7+ you can also work out why he needs a human hand. X4c, X5
  • A Accept, then go straight back to Esteban and talk to him.
    He behaves exactly as before — he was not there and cannot know. Karma dropped 50. X6–X7
  • A Attack him.
    A real fight, and no prison. Three separate wards jail you in vanilla and this fix is on its third attempt — the two before it were byte-verified and still did nothing. If you are arrested, this is the one to report. X8
  • A As a caster, cast near him. Then leave the Crossroads, come back, attack again.
    No jail either time. Every map entry re-clones a warded Esteban, so the fix has to survive that. X8a, X8e
  • B Attack him without the contract.
    Still jailed. Vanilla behaviour is untouched for anyone not on the goblins' payroll. X8d
  • A Kill him, then return to the Patrol Leader, then talk again.
    His quests fail, the Templar step rewinds, karma drops 75. Then 450 gold and the quest completes; the second conversation pays nothing more. X8c, X9–X10
