## The Unholy Oubliette

From play after 0.99.32: *"There's a map, the unholy oubelette. It has a fantastic set piece, but nothing really happens -
is there cut content here?"*

### What is there (measured 2026-11-17, vanilla `Levels/Sewers/03 Unholy Oubliette.zax`, untouched by Fixt)

**No cut content.** Nothing in the map, in the two maps that name it, or in any Sewers tree, quest or template points at
an unfinished scene. The plan already counts it among the silent maps: about 480 entities, 57 generators and no
conversations (`plan.md`, "The dead below").

- **The set piece works.** `Ghoul Male Large Generator` (3433,2923), beside the altar, spawns one Large Ghoul at any mojo.
  Its `After Action` sets the ghoul's death to open `Altar` two seconds later. The altar is a `CDoorAI`, and opening it
  adds a `GetCloseThenTrigger` specifier to `Cross` that fires `Cross relay`: a full heal with
  *"Your health has been replenished"*, or *"You do not require healing"* at full health. It can be used again and again.
- **It is easy to miss.** Nothing announces the altar opening, and the cross looks no different once it is clickable. So
  the reward for clearing the chamber is a healing shrine most players never use, and the room reads as nothing happening.
- **The rest is dressing and combat:**
  - 15 `Undead Generator`s (zombies, skeletons, greater skeletons and terrors, each at three mojo tiers);
  - three `Ghoul Female Generator`s;
  - spirit generators, four of them in a ring round the altar under two `Blue Torch Fire`s;
  - a cold trap and a lightning trap (`CTouchingPolygonTriggerAI` with `CAISecretReveal`);
  - `Hidden Treasure` (a small gold pile) and one chest;
  - `Door2`, which unlocks `Door1`.
- **The one stub is a developer shortcut.** `warp` is an inactive `Editor/Test Interaction` beside the altar, commented
  *"Ghoull Large Dead"*, whose only action is `COpenDoorAction` on `Altar`. It lets a tester skip the ghoul. It is not
  unfinished content.
- **Other maps that name it:**
  - `02 Thieves Congregation` holds the only door in. The map is a dead end.
  - `3 Montaillou/02 Hamlet Burned` expires it with every other sewer map.
- **The altar art is shared with one other map.** The `Transformed Region/Altar` models appear only here and in
  `Levels/Oupost.zax`, which nothing links to and which reads as a tileset test map (five named parts, no story).

### Options

**1. Make the shrine noticeable (small).** When the altar opens:
- a combat log line;
- a short `CAIAttractCamera` pull to the cross (`engine-notes.md` has the pattern);
- a highlight on the cross (`Highlight Self Time` on the specifier it gains).

The timing comes from the existing `After Action` chain. A map edit, so it reaches an Oubliette not yet entered.

**2. "The dead below" (a quest).** From `plan.md`: the sewer trolls dig, the dead are through the wall, and the player goes
down to recover the trolls who did not come back. It gives the map its first conversation and a reason to visit, and the
shrine becomes the place to rest mid-descent. The plan holds it in reserve because the Sewers already lean on clear-the-area
errands. To be worth building, it needs a choice or a consequence, not just a count of the dead. The design work is to find
what the trolls lost down there that matters to them, and what it costs to bring it back.

**3. Use the chamber in the story (open).** The map is a dead end with a holy cross in an unholy crypt, and no one
explains either. Any 2.0 content placed here must fit what the Sewers' factions (thieves, beggars, trolls, wererats)
already say about the tunnels, so read their trees before writing a line.

## Dual wielding

From 2026-11-17: *"could we give the player dual wielding?"*, then *"what if only small weapons could be used in the off
hand, would that balance it?"* and *"what if we added a dual wielding skill?"* Research only; nothing is built.

**The answer is a data-only approximation.** An off-hand weapon goes in the shield slot, and a skill pays for a second
hit that rides on the main hand's swing. The engine has one weapon in combat and no off-hand animations. A true second
weapon, with its own swing, roll and UI, would be exe and animation work, which `design.md` rules out for Fixt.

### What the engine has (measured 2026-11-17, GOG `Lionheart.exe` in Ghidra, vanilla data)

- **A dual-wield type that was never wired up.** `Resources/Melee Dual Weild/` holds four empty `CMeleeDualWieldItem`
  records: `Either`, `Neither`, `Primary` and `Secondary`. `FUN_00490300` registers the folder as `Melee Dual Wield Types`,
  next to `Melee Skill Types`. The class is a 12-byte object with no fields. No data file points at it, and the only code
  that touches its registry (`0x0072dfb8`) or class (`0x0072e058`) is the loader, factory and static-init boilerplate at
  `0x42ebf0`-`0x42ee18` and `0x431200`-`0x4314c0`. It reads as an abandoned "which hand may this weapon go in" setting.
- **The off-hand slot exists, and it is the shield slot.** Shields use `Slot Used In=Character Slot Types/Hand` with
  `Number Hands Needed=One`. Weapons use `Weapon`, and a `Two`-handed weapon is what blocks the Hand slot. The slot names
  mislead: `Hand.CharacterSlotType` is `Name=Hands`, and both `Arm` (bracers) and `Shield` (unused) are `Name=Arms`.
- **A shield is drawn at the Arm attachment.** `CPlugInBehaviorXRPGCharacterCompositeGrannyModelOverride` maps
  `Character Slot Types/Arm` to the shield mesh. An off-hand weapon would use the same override.
- **Combat reads one item.** `FUN_00443220` returns the current weapon: the character profile's slot table at `+0x124`,
  the Weapon slot's list, entry `[profile+0x44]`. `FUN_004433f0` asks that item for its attack type, and the attack paths
  (for example `0x473370`, which calls the weapon's vtable `+0x80` for range) consult it alone.
- **Equipping works on every slot, but only the Weapon slot rearms.** `FUN_00428b30` (select) and `FUN_00428bf0`
  (deselect) set the item's equipped flag at `+0x74` and call its `+0x78` / `+0x7c` hooks. That is what makes
  `CPlugInBehaviorModifyCharacterWhenSelected` work on shields and bracers. Only when the slot at `+0x6c` is Weapon do they
  also call `FUN_0046b470`, which refreshes the attack.
- **On-hit effects come from the weapon.** `CPlugInBehaviorStrikeAction` overrides vtable slot `+0xb0` (`0x5efe00`; base
  `0x50e770` is a no-op), and `FUN_005ef1c0` broadcasts it across one item's behaviours. All 19 vanilla users are
  weapons, weapon additions or wands. **Whether a strike action on a Hand-slot item fires is not confirmed.** Everything
  above suggests it does not, and probe 1 below settles it.
- **No left-hand attack animation exists.** Player attack sequences are `AttackOneHanded`, `AttackTwoHanded`, `AttackBow`,
  `AttackCrossBow` and `AttackPunch`, all right-handed.

### Weapon damage, and why "small weapons only" is not enough alone

Every vanilla weapon uses one formula: a dice roll, plus **+1 per 50 points** of its melee skill (to +6 at 300), plus
strength scaling and any magic additions.

| Weapon | Hands | Weight | Dice | Average |
|---|---|---:|---|---:|
| Short Sword | One | 4 | 1-5 | 3 |
| Club | One | 4 | 1-6 | 3.5 |
| Scimitar | One | 6 | 1-6 | 3.5 |
| Mace | One | 6 | 2-7 | 4.5 |
| Long Sword | One | 6 | 1-8 | 4.5 |
| Morning Star | One | 7 | 2-7 | 4.5 |
| Bastard Sword | One | 10 | 1-10 | 5.5 |
| Great Mace | Two | 11 | 3-10 | 6.5 |
| Two-Handed Sword, Battle Axe, War Hammer | Two | 9-10 | 2-12 | 7 |
| Great Two-Handed Sword | Two | 12 | 4-14 | 9 |

There is no dagger. The Short Sword also carries +20% `Attack Animation Speed Multiplier`.

A size limit only caps the dice, and late in the game the dice are the small part:
- **The flat skill bonus dominates.** With a Long Sword and a Short Sword, two full hits at skill 300 come to about
  (4.5+6) + (3+6) = 19.5 per swing, against about 13 for a Two-Handed Sword.
- **Magic additions are size-blind.** A Short Sword of Lightning Strike procs as often as a Bastard Sword of it, so an
  off-hand with its own additions doubles the proc chances.

**The size rule: weight 6 or less.** Strictly small (Short Sword and Club) leaves two base weapons, which is too thin.
Weight 6 or less adds the Scimitar and the Mace, gives a slashing and a crushing choice for each hand, and still excludes
the Long Sword, Morning Star and Bastard Sword.

**The shield is the built-in price.** A Small Shield is +2 AC and 3% physical resistances, a Medium Shield +4 AC and 5%.
Dual wielding should sit between the two-hander (biggest single hits) and sword and shield (most defence).

### The Dual Wield skill

Skills are data. `OneHandedMelee.Skill` is a `Parent Skill`, a `Sort Priority`, a range of -300 to 300, an
`Initial Value` formula and an `Image`. Nothing in the exe lists the skills, and the character creator offers any skill
with `Can be selected as Tag Skill=1`.

**Adding a skill is structurally safe.** 0.33.0's `ENEMY Healing` was the 93rd `.Skill`. `EH5` (passed 2026-10-10)
confirmed that `Skill Values` is keyed by full path with no `Item Count`, so a skill a character has never stored reads 0.
Existing saves therefore start at 0, which is the intended starting value anyway.

**There are two ways to build it:**

| | A. A normal skill under Fight (6th, next to One-Handed) | B. A child of One-Handed (`Value Type=Parents value minus differential`, as Barter follows Diplomacy) |
|---|---|---|
| Cost | Skill points: a real build choice | Free; it rises with One-Handed |
| Old saves | Starts at 0 | Works at once |
| Balance lever | Strong | Weak; it is "One-Handed minus N" |
| UI risk | A 6th row under Fight | Nested under One-Handed |

**Choose A**, with `Initial Value` 0 and `Can be selected as Tag Skill=0` to begin with. A tag skill would make it cheap
from character creation, and leaving it off keeps it off the creator screen until it has been seen there.

**What the skill pays for:**
- **The equip gate.** The off-hand item's `Requirements` array asks for Dual Wield 50, which keeps it out of the opening
  hours.
- **How often the off-hand lands.** The second hit fires on a landed main-hand swing (`CExpressionHitMargin > 0`) with a
  chance that rises with the skill, for example 25% low to about 75% at 300.
- **A small flat bonus.** +1 per 100, at most +3, so it never matches the main hand's +6.
- **Nothing else.** The off-hand rolls its own dice plus that bonus: no One-Handed bonus, no strength.

At skill 300 that is about 0.75 x (3 + 3) = +4.5 per swing over the main hand. That puts Long Sword plus Short Sword at
roughly a two-hander, bought with skill points and the shield.

### How it would be built

1. **Off-hand items.** Copies of the base weapons of weight 6 or less, with `Slot Used In=Hand`,
   `Number Hands Needed=One`, the Dual Wield requirement, and a model override that puts the blade on `Arm`. While
   selected, each raises a derived attribute flag, a new derived attribute with the same keyed-map risk as the 130th
   added for the shield set bonus.
2. **The second hit.** Every base `Weapon` item gains a `CPlugInBehaviorStrikeAction`: if the swing landed, the flag is
   set and the skill roll passes, `CActionDoDamage` deals the off-hand's dice plus the bonus. The off-hand's dice and
   damage type must be readable from the main hand, either one flag per off-hand base or a fixed table.
3. **Off-hand additions.** Decide whether magic additions on an off-hand item proc. The simplest rule is that they never
   do, because the strike action lives on the main hand.
4. **The mesh.** The Arm mount is a forearm shield mount, so each off-hand mesh needs rotating into the hand (in-place
   vertex transform, as 0.89.0 did for two weapons). The angle is unknown until tested.
5. **The skill.** `Skills/Fighting/DualWield.Skill`, plus an `.mdl16` icon from ModTools' icon tool.

**Limits to state up front:**
- The character still swings right-handed only.
- There is no separate roll or UI line for the off-hand.
- Saves keep full item copies, so weapons already in a save do not gain the strike action; only weapons dropped or bought
  after the update do.
- Enemies and companions are untouched.

### Probes before any design work (one session)

1. **Does a strike action fire from the Hand slot?** If it does, the second hit can live on the off-hand item instead,
   which removes the save-copy limit and the need for a flag.
2. **The Arm mount.** Equip a weapon mesh through the shield override and see how it sits.
3. **A visible new skill (the 94th).** `ENEMY Healing` was hidden, so no new skill has been seen on the player's sheet. Check the
   skill window and the level-up screen with a 6th Fight skill: it shows, points can be spent, and nothing shifts. The
   Thief branch already shows six entries, so it probably fits.
4. **The requirement.** An off-hand Short Sword needing the test skill refuses to equip below it and equips above it.

## Combat abilities

From 2026-11-17: *"what if we wanted to add subskills under the combat ones, ie a mighty sweep for 2 hand that hits all
enemies around, or a shield bash that knocks down enemies. Essentially spell equivalents for the combat skills?"*, then
*"i think they might still cost mana, but not all of them."* Research only; nothing is built.

**The answer is yes, as spells built from combat parts.** Casters have dozens of active spells, and fighters have no
active abilities at all. Almost nothing about a spell is tied to magic, so a combat ability is a spell skill parented
under a combat skill.

### How a spell is put together (measured 2026-11-17, vanilla `.Skill` files and `Lionheart.exe`)

- **Unlocking and scaling are one mechanism.** Every spell is a child of a school skill with
  `Value Type=Parents value minus differential`, and the differential is its unlock threshold. `Fire Circle` is
  `Parent Skill=Skills/Magic Thought/Fire`, `Value Differential=49`: it appears at Fire 50 and grows with Fire. A
  Two-Handed ability is the same shape: `Parent Skill=Skills/Fighting/TwoHandedMelee` with a differential of, say, 75.
- **A spell need not sit in a magic tree.** The five `Fake Wand Spells` are real spells (three carry
  `CPlugInBehaviorSpell`) parented under `Skills/Thieving/Barter`, and `Lionheart.exe` names no skill tree at all. As far
  as the data shows, carrying `CPlugInBehaviorSpell` is what makes a skill castable. Not yet proven for a skill the player
  casts directly; probe 1 below.
- **A spell is a stack of generic behaviours.** `Fire Circle.Skill` carries five:
  - `CPlugInBehaviorSpell`: `ManaUse` (an expression), `Duration`, and
    `Automatically disable recasting of spell for duration`, which is a ready-made cooldown;
  - `CPlugInBehaviorPlaySequence`: any `Sequence Name` (`SpellCast` there; `AttackTwoHanded`, `AttackOneHanded`,
    `AttackPunch` and `AttackBow` all exist on the player);
  - `CPlugInBehaviorAttack`: `Max Attack Range`, `Is Location Based`;
  - `CXRPGPlugInBehaviorLauncher`: what it fires, how many (`Num To Launch`), the spread (`Dispersion Angle`), and
    `Primary Launchable Slot`, which is how bows fire from the `Arrow` slot;
  - `CPlugInBehaviorLaunchAction`: any action on launch.
- **Area hits exist.** `Fire Circle Instant Hit Projectile` spawns a `CAIAreaOvalManager` whose `Radius` is a named
  expression on the skill. Centred on the player with a melee-sized radius, that is a sweep.
- **Knockdown exists, as data.** `Critical Hits/Knocked Down.CriticalHitEffect.txt` is a `CPlugInBehaviorStrikeAction`
  that runs `CAssignTemporaryTaskAction` on `$Trigger`: play `Death` at speed 2, then `GetUp`, then
  `CActionRemoveCriticalHits` on itself. `Knocked Back` is the same at speed 7 and 1.5. The exe has a
  `Critical Hits/Can Be Knocked Around` category that decides who can be knocked about.
- **Stun exists.** `Rigor Mortis` assigns a temporary `CWaitAI` task through `CAssignTemporaryTaskAction`.
- **No expression reads the equipped weapon.** No class tests what is in the Weapon or Hand slot, and nothing reads the
  current weapon's dice.

### Cost: some cost mana, some do not

The cost lives in `ManaUse`, an expression per ability, so each one can be set on its own. `ManaUse=0` is not yet proven
to work (probe 1). The cooldown comes from `Duration` plus the recast lockout, and every ability gets one.

**The rule:**
- **Cooldown only** for the basic move of each weapon style: what a trained fighter can always do.
- **Mana plus cooldown** for the big moves: area hits, crowd control, and anything that changes a fight.

The mana costs stay low, because fighter builds have little mana. Spending mana on an ability then competes with
potions and with any spells the character has. That gives hybrid builds a real choice, and a pure fighter can still use
the cooldown-only moves all fight.

### Five abilities per combat skill, like the spell schools

From 2026-11-17: *"we would want 4-5 abilities per each combat tree, like spells"*.

**How the spell schools are laid out.** Each of the 12 schools (four each under Divine, Thought and Tribal) has 4 or 5
spells. The nine five-spell schools use differentials **0, 24, 49, 74, 109**, so the spells unlock at school skill
**1, 25, 50, 75 and 110**. The three four-spell schools (Thought Fire, Tribal Domination, Divine Defensive) use
**0, 24, 49, 89**.

**The combat ladder has to start higher.** Magic schools start at **0** (`Fire.Skill` has `Initial Value` 0), but every
combat skill starts at twice one attribute plus another: One-Handed is 2 AG + ST, Two-Handed 2 ST + AG, Ranged 2 AG +
PE, Unarmed 2 AG + PE, Evasion 2 PE + LK. A new character sits around 15-30 in all five. Copied as-is, the spell ladder
would hand every character, mages included, the first ability of all five trees at creation.

**So keep the spell spacing and shift it up:** unlock at **35, 55, 80, 105 and 135**, which is differentials **34, 54,
79, 104 and 134**. A specialist reaches the first ability in the first act and the last late in the game; a character
who never invests never gets past the first one or two.

**The roster** (25 abilities; **C** = cooldown only, **M** = mana and cooldown). Every effect is built from a part named
in "How a spell is put together" above: the area oval, the launcher, `Knocked Down` / `Knocked Back`, the `CWaitAI` stun,
`CXRPGDamageOverTime`, timed modifiers through `CAddCharacterModifierToCharacterAction`, `CGiveHealthToCharacterAction`,
and `CExpressionHealthPercent`.

That is 13 cooldown-only and 12 mana abilities. Every tree opens with a cooldown move, so a pure fighter always has
something to press. The mana moves are the area, crowd-control and finishing abilities.

**A charge is left out on purpose.** `CChargeAttackAI` exists in the exe but no vanilla data uses it, and
`CTeleportAction` only moves to a named map marker (its two vanilla uses are the Crypt teleporters). If a probe shows
`CChargeAttackAI` can be given to the player as a temporary task, **Charge** could replace Heavy Blow or Sprint.

The names, thresholds, costs and cooldowns are starting values for play to correct, not balance.

**Build it in waves.** 25 abilities means 25 icons, 25 descriptions and about ten new area or projectile launchables.
Wave 1 is the first two tiers of every tree (10 abilities), which covers every mechanism above except the health-percent
finisher. Waves 2 and 3 add the rest once wave 1 plays well.

### Design rules

1. **Weapon requirements need a flag.** Two-handers, shields, one-handers, bows and crossbows, and the `Unarmed` item
   each raise a derived attribute while selected (base weapons
   already carry a when-selected block; the Short Sword's +20% speed lives there), and the ability's
   `Display and Cast Requirements` checks it. Without the gate, a player could sweep with a Short Sword. **The save
   caveat applies:** a weapon or shield already in a save is a full copy and will not raise the flag until replaced.
   The flags can be shared with dual wielding's off-hand flag.
2. **Damage follows the skill, not the weapon.** Nothing can read the equipped weapon's dice, so each ability carries
   its own formula on its parent skill. A Great Sword and a Battle Axe sweep for the same amount.
3. **Stay out of magic.** `Fire Circle` sets `Game Scripting Variables/Player has cast a spell`; combat abilities must
   not. They also take no part in `Thought Spell Cast Multiplier` or any other magic perk or multiplier.
4. **Respect who can be knocked down.** Shield Bash checks `Can Be Knocked Around` before applying the sequence, and only
   uses it on creatures with a `GetUp` animation.
5. **Enemies can use them too.** Enemies already cast spells through `CActionSelectSkill`. An English knight with Shield
   Bash or an orc chief with Mighty Sweep would change how fights open (see the enemy behaviour ceiling in
   `engine-notes.md`). Remember that `CActionSelectSkill` also chooses the next attack, so an enemy given an ability must
   actually have it.
6. **Build in waves** (above). Wave 2 waits until wave 1 feels right in play.

### What each ability needs

- A `.Skill` file under `Resources/Skills/Fighting/<parent>/`, with `Value Type=Parents value minus differential`,
  `Can be selected as Tag Skill=0`, and its numbers in its own `Expressions` array, read with
  `CSkillsNamedExpressionExpression` (`engine-notes.md` explains why not `CInventoryAdditionsNamedExpressionExpression`).
- An `.mdl16` icon from ModTools' icon tool.
- For an area or projectile ability, a launchable under `Spell Projectiles/`.
- A display name and description that read as a combat move, not a spell.

### Probes before any design work (one session)

1. **Can it be cast?** A test ability under Two-Handed Melee, with `ManaUse=0` and a cooldown: does it appear in the skill
   window, take a hotkey, and cast for a character with no magic skill? Then the same with a small mana cost.
2. **The animation.** Does `PlaySequence` with `AttackTwoHanded` play from a skill?
3. **The knockdown.** Does `Knocked Down` work when fired from a skill's launch action rather than a critical hit, and
   which common creatures lack a `GetUp` animation?
4. **The gate.** Does a flag requirement hide the ability, or block its cast, when the flag is down?
5. **The cooldown.** Does the recast lockout show the player that the ability is unavailable, and does it stop a
   hotkey cast?
